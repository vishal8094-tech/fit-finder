Why does the same size fit differently everywhere?

It's not you — it's the industry. Here's what's actually going on behind every size label.

Vanity sizing is real

Many brands quietly widen their size charts over the years so a "medium" feels more flattering than it used to. That's why a medium from one label can be a small — or a large — somewhere else. The label is marketing as much as it is measurement.

Cut philosophy differs by brand

A "fitted" brand and a "relaxed" brand can use the exact same size label for garments with several centimetres of difference in ease. That's the gap Fit Finder is built to close — by comparing your real measurement, not the label.

Fabric changes the fit

Stretch fabrics (like most jersey knits) forgive a size or two. Rigid fabrics (raw denim, structured cotton) don't. Two "medium" tops in different fabrics can sit completely differently on the same body.

Region changes the chart

The same global brand often ships different size charts for different markets. A US-cut and an EU-cut of the "same" item are rarely identical — always check the region the chart was built for.

3. How to Measure Your Body3. Body का सही माप कैसे लें

Chest / Bust — Measure around the fullest part, keeping the tape horizontal and comfortably snug.सबसे चौड़े हिस्से के चारों ओर tape रखें। Tape सीधी और comfortably snug रखें।

Waist — Measure around the narrowest part of your waist without pulling the tape tight.कमर के सबसे संकरे हिस्से के चारों ओर माप लें और tape को बहुत tight न खींचें।

Hip — For a saved Full Body Profile, measure around the fullest part of your hips with the tape level.Full Body Profile में hips के सबसे चौड़े हिस्से के चारों ओर सीधी tape से माप लें।

Shoulder — Measure across the shoulder area as requested by the profile form, keeping the tape level.Profile form के अनुसार shoulder area का माप लें और tape को सीधा रखें।

Foot length — Stand with your heel against a wall and measure from the heel to your longest toe. Measuring in the evening can be useful because feet may be slightly larger.एड़ी दीवार से लगाकर heel से सबसे लंबे toe तक मापें। शाम को पैर थोड़ा बड़ा हो सकता है, इसलिए उस समय मापना उपयोगी हो सकता है।

Keep the tape relaxed: not squeezing the body and not hanging loose.Tape को न बहुत tight रखें और न बहुत loose — body के साथ आराम से रखें।
