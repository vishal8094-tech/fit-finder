📐 How to Measure Correctly

Chest/Bust: Measure around the fullest part, under the arms, tape horizontal and snug.
Waist: Measure around the narrowest part without pulling tight.
Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips with tape level.
Foot length: Heel against wall, measure to longest toe. Evening is best.

Keep the tape relaxed — not squeezing and not hanging loose.

Why does the same size fit differently everywhere?

🏷️ Vanity sizing is real

Many brands quietly widen their size charts over the years so a "medium" feels more flattering. A medium from one label can be a small — or a large — somewhere else.

✂️ Cut philosophy differs

A "fitted" brand and a "relaxed" brand can use the exact same size label for garments with several centimetres of difference in ease.

🧵 Fabric changes the fit

Stretch fabrics forgive a size or two. Rigid fabrics don't. Two "medium" tops in different fabrics can sit completely differently.

🌍 Region changes the chart

A US-cut and an EU-cut of the "same" item are rarely identical — always check the region the chart was built for.

Fit Finder कैसे काम करता है — A simple user guide

1. How Fit Finder Works ▼
Fit Finder compares your body measurement with the size bands used by selected category and brands, then applies your fit preference. Recommendations are estimates — actual fit can vary by brand, cut, fabric, and body shape.
2. Getting Started ▼
Select a category → Enter measurement → Select CM/Inch → Choose fit preference → Click "Find my size" → View results. Example: Shirt, chest 92 cm, Regular fit → Recommended: M.
3. How to Measure Your Body ▼
Chest: Fullest part under arms. Waist: Narrowest part. Hip: Fullest part. Shoulder: Across shoulder area. Foot: Heel to longest toe. Keep tape relaxed.
4. CM & Inch Guide ▼
76 cm ≈ 29.9 in | 80 cm ≈ 31.5 in | 92 cm ≈ 36.2 in | 100 cm ≈ 39.4 in | 106 cm ≈ 41.7 in
5. Fit Preference Guide ▼
Loose: Roomy, extra space. Regular: Balanced, everyday. Tight: Fitted, closer to body. This is a preference, not a guarantee.
6. Full Body Profile ▼
Save measurements in a profile → Select profile later → Fit Finder auto-fills. Multiple profiles supported for family members.
7. Privacy & Data ▼
Profiles stored in browser local storage only. Not sent to any server. Use the Backup feature to save your data externally.
