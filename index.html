Why does the same size fit differently everywhere?

It's not you — it's the industry. Here's what's actually going on behind every size label.

🏷️ Vanity sizing is real

Many brands quietly widen their size charts over the years so a "medium" feels more flattering. That's why a medium from one label can be a small — or a large — somewhere else.

✂️ Cut philosophy differs by brand

A "fitted" brand and a "relaxed" brand can use the exact same size label for garments with several centimetres of difference in ease.

🧵 Fabric changes the fit

Stretch fabrics forgive a size or two. Rigid fabrics don't. Two "medium" tops in different fabrics can sit completely differently.

🌍 Region changes the chart

The same global brand often ships different size charts for different markets. A US-cut and an EU-cut of the "same" item are rarely identical.

Fit Finder Help Center

A simple user guide for finding clothing sizes, measuring correctly, saving profiles and understanding recommendations.

1. How Fit Finder Works ▼
Fit Finder helps you find a suitable clothing size based on your body measurement, selected clothing category, brand sizing information and preferred fit. Size recommendations are estimates. Actual fit can vary by brand, product cut, fabric, size chart, region and individual body shape.
2. Getting Started ▼

01 Select a category
02 Enter your measurement
03 Select CM or Inch
04 Select your fit preference
05 Click "Find my size"
06 View the recommended sizes

Example: For a shirt, if your chest is 92 cm and you prefer Regular fit, select Shirts, enter 92, keep CM selected, choose Regular and calculate your size.

3. How to Measure Your Body ▼

Chest / Bust — Measure around the fullest part, keeping the tape horizontal and comfortably snug.

Waist — Measure around the narrowest part of your waist without pulling the tape tight.

Hip — Measure around the fullest part of your hips with the tape level.

Shoulder — Measure across the shoulder area, keeping the tape level.

Foot length — Stand with your heel against a wall and measure from the heel to your longest toe. Measuring in the evening can be useful because feet may be slightly larger.

Keep the tape relaxed: not squeezing the body and not hanging loose.

4. CM & Inch Guide ▼

Fit Finder supports both centimetres (cm) and inches (in). Select your preferred unit and enter your measurement in that unit.

5. Fit Preference Guide ▼

LOOSE — More relaxed and roomy. Useful if you prefer extra space.

REGULAR — Balanced and comfortable for everyday wear.

TIGHT — More fitted and closer to the body.

Your fit preference can influence the recommended size. It is a preference setting, not a guarantee of exact fit.

6. Size Calculator Guide ▼

Category → Measurement → Unit → Fit → Calculate → Recommended Size

For example, the result may say "Recommended Size: M". This means M is the estimated match for the information you entered and the brand/category data used by the tool.

7. Full Body Profile ▼

Save your measurements once in a profile, then select that profile later so Fit Finder can fill the relevant measurement automatically.

  • Create a profile such as "My Profile".
  • Save the available body measurements.
  • Select the saved profile for another category.
  • Edit measurements whenever they change.

Multiple profiles are supported, so you can keep separate profiles for family members.

8. Men's Clothing Categories ▼

Shirts • Jeans • T-Shirts • Hoodies • Trousers • Shoes

Shirts, T-shirts and hoodies use chest; jeans and trousers use waist; shoes use foot length. Always check the product's official chart before buying.

9. Women's Clothing Categories ▼

Dresses / Tops • Co-ord Sets • Kurtis • Salwar Suits • Palazzo / Pants

Dresses / Tops and the listed ethnic categories use bust for the calculator; Palazzo / Pants uses waist. Use the measurement guidance above and check the product chart before purchase.

10. Brand Sizing Guide ▼

Different brands can use different size charts, cuts, fit philosophies, fabrics and regional standards. The same person may be recommended M by one brand and L by another.

Fit Finder is a comparison aid, not a replacement for the current official product size chart.

15. Size Recommendation Disclaimer ▼

Fit Finder provides size recommendations for guidance. Actual fit may vary depending on the brand, product design, fabric, cut, size chart and individual body shape.

16. Frequently Asked Questions ▼

How does Fit Finder calculate my size?
It compares your entered measurement with the size bands used by the selected category and brands, then applies your fit preference.

How do I measure my chest?
Measure around the fullest part of your chest, under the arms, keeping the tape horizontal and comfortably snug.

Should I enter measurements in cm or inches?
Either is fine. Select cm or in and enter the measurement in that unit.

What is Loose, Regular and Tight fit?
Loose is roomier, Regular is balanced, and Tight is closer to the body. The choice can influence the recommendation.

Can I save my measurements?
Yes. Use Full Body Profile to save measurements locally in your browser.

Can I create profiles for family members?
Yes. The site supports multiple saved profiles.

Why can my size differ between brands?
Brand charts, cuts, fabrics, fit and regional standards can differ.

Is Fit Finder free?
The basic size calculator is free.

Are Fit Finder recommendations 100% accurate?
No. They are estimates for guidance and actual fit can vary.

What should I do if the recommended size doesn't fit?
Check the retailer's current official size chart and product details, and use the site's feedback option to report the real-world fit.
