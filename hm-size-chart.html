Does H&M run large or small?

H&M generally runs true to size with a relaxed, comfortable cut — noticeably roomier than fitted brands like Zara at the same letter size. If you prefer a slimmer silhouette, sizing down by one is common. For structured pieces like jackets and jeans, stick closer to your measured size.

Frequently asked questions

Does H&M run large or small?
H&M generally has a relaxed cut and tends to run slightly large compared to fitted brands like Zara. Most shoppers find their true size works well, and some prefer sizing down for a slimmer fit.
What is H&M size M in cm?
For H&M men's tops, size M typically corresponds to a chest measurement of roughly 98 to 103 cm. For women's tops, size M typically corresponds to a bust measurement of roughly 90 to 94 cm. Always check the specific product's size chart, since it can vary by style.
Is H&M sizing the same as Indian sizing?
Not exactly, but H&M's relaxed European cut is generally closer to Indian domestic sizing than fitted brands like Zara. Comparing your body measurement against the size chart is still the most reliable way to choose.

These charts are approximate and reviewed periodically against publicly available H&M sizing information. Always check the product's official size chart before you buy — actual fit varies by style, fabric and cut.
