Why shoe sizes vary between brands

Shoe sizes aren't fully standardised across countries or brands. The "last" — the foot-shaped mold a shoe is built on — differs by manufacturer, so the same labeled size can run narrow, wide, long or short depending on the brand.

How to measure your foot at home

  • Place a sheet of paper on a hard floor, against a wall.
  • Stand on the paper with your heel touching the wall.
  • Mark the tip of your longest toe on the paper.
  • Measure the distance from the wall edge to the mark, in centimetres.
  • Repeat for both feet — most people have one foot slightly larger. Size for the larger foot.
Tip: measure your feet in the evening, when they're typically at their largest, and wear the socks you'd normally wear with that type of shoe.

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure my foot size at home?

Stand on a sheet of paper against a wall, mark the tip of your longest toe and the back of your heel, then measure the distance in centimetres.

Why do shoe sizes differ between brands?

Shoe lasts (the mold a shoe is built on) vary by brand and region, so the same labeled size can fit differently in width and length.

Should I measure both feet?

Yes. Most people have one foot slightly larger than the other. Measure both and size for the larger foot.
