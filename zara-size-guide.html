Does Zara run small?

Yes, generally. Zara's cut leans fitted and European, so most people size up compared to relaxed brands like H&M. If your measurement falls right on the line between two sizes on the charts above, go with the larger size — especially for structured fabrics like denim or blazers, which have less give than jersey or knit fabrics.

Frequently asked questions

Does Zara run small?
Zara tends to have a fitted, European cut, so many shoppers find it runs slightly smaller than brands like H&M. If your measurement sits between two sizes, sizing up is usually the safer choice.
What is Zara size M in cm?
For Zara men's tops, size M typically corresponds to a chest measurement of roughly 96 to 101 cm. For women's tops, size M typically corresponds to a bust measurement of roughly 88 to 92 cm. Always check the specific product's size chart, since it can vary by style.
Is Zara sizing the same as Indian sizing?
No. Zara uses European size logic, which generally runs narrower through the chest and shoulders than typical Indian domestic brands at the same letter size. Comparing your body measurement, not the label, is the most reliable way to convert.
